# Reset

**Household routines that actually happen**

> #19 of 32 · Wave 2: Recurring keepers · Used: Daily/weekly · Harder · Revenue potential: Medium · Home & Household

## What it is

Short, repeatable cleaning and household routines: a daily 10-minute reset, a Sunday reset, laundry, bins and seasonal tasks, room by room. Share the household's tasks and assign them.

## Why it's in the portfolio

A durable weekly problem with a big social-media audience (#CleanTok, 'Sunday reset'). Shared households bring more than one user per home and justify a subscription.

## Who it's for

Renters, parents, couples, students, anyone overwhelmed by housework.

**The problem:** Cleaning piles up without a routine, and splitting it in a household causes friction.

## Version one

- Room-by-room checklists
- Daily 10-minute and weekly reset routines
- Laundry, bins and seasonal reminders
- Tick off and see progress per room
- Optional before/after photos kept on the device

**Example:** A parent opens Reset on Sunday, does a 20-minute kitchen reset and assigns the bins to their partner.

## Later

- Shared household and task assignment (needs sync)
- Household packs: move-in, deep clean, spring clean
- Custom routines

## Animation and tone: five signature animations

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- Task ticked: the room sparkles
- Sunday reset: the stickman sweeps in cartoon fast-forward
- Laundry done: the socks pair up and hold hands
- Room spotless: the stickman slides across the floor in socks
- Chore shared: a paper plane delivers it to a housemate
